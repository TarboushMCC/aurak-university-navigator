Select: click a shape to select, drag its white handles to reshape, drag the shape body to move it, drag the red handle to rotate. Rectangle/Circle: click-drag to draw. Polygon: click to add each corner, then click "✓ Finish shape". Path: click along a walkway to drop a chain of nodes (clicking near an existing node reuses it, so branching paths connect), then click "✓ Finish path". Set each node's kind (gate, entrance, junction, etc.) in the Walkway nodes list. Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan. Click a shape/node in its list to focus it.
